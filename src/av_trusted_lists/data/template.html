EU age verification: providers by country

The EU age-verification scheme lets a person prove to a website that they are over a given age. The proof is issued by a provider that an EU member state has authorised. Member states notify their providers to the European Commission, which publishes them in one signed file, the Age Verification Trusted List. A website that follows the scheme uses the list to decide which providers' proofs to accept.

The Commission publishes a second list for integration testing. This page reads both lists once a day and shows which countries have a provider on each.

A listing records that a member state has notified the Commission of a provider. Whether an app is available to the public in that country, and whether any website accepts it, is outside what the lists record. The terms used on this page are defined under Terms.

Terms

Trusted list
The Age Verification Trusted List. Its legal notice reads: "The present list contains information notified by Member States on the Proof of Age Attestation Providers and the Proof of Age Services they provide for the purpose of facilitating compliance of online services with Article 28 of the Digital Services Act."
Test list
The list the Commission publishes in the acceptance environment of its eIDAS Dashboard. Its legal notice reads: "The present list contains TEST information notified by Member States on the Proof of Age Attestation Providers and the Proof of Age Services they provide for the purpose of integration testing". The entry under Iceland is the reference implementation of the age-verification project.
Provider
A Proof of Age Attestation Provider, an organisation that issues proofs of age. A provider has one or more services on a list. Each service has a status and a certificate.
Country
The member state in which the provider is established, as the list states it.
recognized, deprecated
The two status values the Commission's specification of the list permits. A country is coloured on the map when one of its services has the status recognized.
Status since
The time the list states for the service's current status.
Certificate validity
The period the listed certificate states. A certificate whose period ended before the build date has the state expired.

Dates are UTC.
